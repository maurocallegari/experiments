# Stealth homepage
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
WordPress, Enfold; standalone PHP plugin, isolated CSS, vanilla JavaScript.

## Users
Owners and staff of small businesses with repeated manual data/document handoffs.

## Product Purpose
Explain how Stealth observes real work, builds useful software and integrations, and evolves them with the client. Invite a concrete discussion through the existing contact page.

## Constraints
User has approved direct development without Superdesign and without another design questionnaire. Existing Enfold header/footer and typography remain. White, black, original light cyan. Consistent wireframe elements, no mascot or stock photography. No duplicate hero, decorative cards, numbered eyebrows or added green. Horizontal story should feel continuous. Mobile content must remain readable at increased text sizes. Keep WordPress copy editable. No invented customers or metrics.

## Evidence
The examples are illustrative workflows, not customer case studies. The old plugin and prototypes are rejected implementation references. No Enfold source or PHP runtime was initially present in this workspace.
